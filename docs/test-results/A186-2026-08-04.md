## Fixed/decided

- REJECTED: panel-side post-display-on settle is not the cause of the wake
  rainbow garbage (A186), just as the off-path ELVSS prep was not (A185).
- The evidence stack now points at the DSI link itself: garbage is
  wake-only, is "rainbow" (HS data corruption), LP-mode commands land
  fine, and no controller-level error is logged.
- Divergence found: downstream ships `qcom,mdss-dsi-force-clock-lane-hs`
  (continuous HS clock lane) while mainline sets
  `MIPI_DSI_CLOCK_NON_CONTINUOUS`. After wake, the non-continuous clock
  lane must re-enter HS for every frame burst; a marginal turn-around
  corrupts the first HS frames until the link stabilizes.
- A187 (in progress): drop `MIPI_DSI_CLOCK_NON_CONTINUOUS` for continuous
  clock, matching Android's shipped behavior. Branch
  `joan/a187-panel-continuous-clk`.
